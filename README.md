# Market Entry Analysis for ABG Motors in India

## Project Overview

The **Market Entry Analysis for ABG Motors in India** is a data analytics project focused on analysing the Indian automobile market and identifying potential business opportunities for market expansion.

The project uses Indian and Japanese automobile customer datasets to understand customer demographics, income distribution, purchasing behaviour, and potential customer segments. Data analysis and predictive modelling techniques are applied to generate meaningful business insights that can support strategic market-entry decisions.

## Project Objectives

- Analyse Indian and Japanese automobile customer datasets.
- Perform data cleaning and preprocessing.
- Understand customer demographics and purchasing patterns.
- Identify potential customer segments based on age, gender, and income.
- Apply Logistic Regression to predict customer purchase probabilities.
- Develop interactive Tableau dashboards.
- Generate data-driven insights to support market-entry strategies.

## Datasets Used

**1. Indian Customer Dataset**
- Total Records: 70,000
- Customer Age
- Gender
- Annual Income
- Customer Information

**2. Japanese Customer Dataset**
- Total Records: 40,000
- Customer Age
- Gender
- Annual Income
- Age of Car
- Purchase Information

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Collection and Understanding
Collected and explored Indian and Japanese automobile customer datasets to understand their structure and identify relevant variables.

### 2. Data Cleaning and Preprocessing
- Checked missing values.
- Identified duplicate records.
- Validated data consistency.
- Prepared datasets for analysis.

### 3. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
Performed analysis to understand:
- Age-wise customer distribution.
- Gender-wise customer distribution.
- Annual income distribution.
- Customer purchasing behaviour.
- Relationship between customer characteristics and purchasing decisions.

### 4. Predictive Modelling – Logistic Regression
Developed a Logistic Regression model using Japanese customer data.

- Divided the dataset into training and testing sets.
- Estimated customer purchase probabilities.
- Evaluated model performance using a confusion matrix.
- Calculated Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-score and Specificity.

### 5. Data Visualization using Tableau
Created visualizations and dashboards to present:
- Gender-wise customer distribution.
- Age group analysis.
- Income distribution.
- Purchase behaviour.
- Customer segmentation and market insights.

## Key Findings
